Service Overview

NyayaSetu provides citizen guidance for lodging grievances with verified public authorities and official destinations.

How It Works

Our process is simple: submit your request, our experts assess your needs, provide tailored solutions, implement the service efficiently, and ensure satisfaction, making technology support seamless, reliable, and hassle-free.

1. Problem Description

Describe your public service issue in plain language or select from curated categories.

2. Situation Mapping

Answer a few guided questions to map your situation to the relevant government authority.

3. Authority & Portal Recommendation

Receive the verified official portal link, relevant department, and authority details.

4. Evidence & Document Checklist

Review the required evidence, receipts, order IDs, or photos to keep ready before acting.

5. Request Draft & Local Tracking

Generate a structured request draft and save a private local tracking record in your browser.

Benefits for Indian Citizens & Communities

NyayaSetu empowers citizens with clarity, saving time and reducing confusion when accessing public grievance portals and government schemes.

  • Reduces confusion around government grievance mechanisms and portal selection.
  • Saves time by providing document checklists before you begin lodging a complaint.
  • Helps structure your grievance clearly with copyable request drafts.
  • Provides private, offline-first local tracking without requiring account creation.
  • Guides citizens strictly to verified official government portals.
